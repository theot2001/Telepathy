Our Story

Founded by Identical Twins. Driven by Shared Understanding.

When two identical twins dedicate their careers to education, the result is a seamless, intuitive approach to how students think, learn, and overcome academic hurdles.

Why Identical Twins Make Better Educators

Growing up side by side, we developed an instinctive ability to understand different perspectives and communicate complex ideas effortlessly. In tuition, "shared understanding" is everything: a student only progresses when the tutor truly sees the problem through the student's eyes.

When one explanation doesn't land, we immediately pivot to an alternative mental model—combining classroom pedagogy with two decades of one-to-one tutoring insight.

Co-Founder 1

Qualified Secondary Teacher (QTS)

Brings up-to-date UK classroom expertise, deep curriculum knowledge across KS3 and KS4 Science and Maths, and insider understanding of how examiners award marks and address common student misconceptions.

Co-Founder 2

Veteran Specialist Tutor

Brings years of dedicated one-to-one coaching across KS2, KS3, and GCSE, specialising in confidence-building, structured revision routines, and adapting pacing to suit every individual learner.
