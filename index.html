About Us

Identical Twins. Two Angles on Every Problem.

Telepathy Tuition was built around a simple advantage: two identical twins who have spent a combined 20 years teaching Maths and Science.

Why Learn With Twins?

Growing up identical, you quickly notice how two people can look at the exact same problem and picture it completely differently. That instinct sits at the heart of how we teach.

When a student gets stuck, it is rarely because they aren't capable—it is usually because the explanation hasn't matched how they think yet. Having two complementary teaching styles means we can switch mental models on the spot until a topic clicks.
